## How sign-off works

- A source-parity check proves preservation, not translation quality or legal accuracy.
- Native Spanish reviewer: approve or mark exact edits by page/key; review in context, including mobile layouts, forms, metadata, links and alt text. Full-page text capture is not a completed linguistic review.
- Owner: approve factual/business answers, final EN/ES wording and each source-copy change. Any Spanish correction remains proposed until explicitly approved.
- Privacy/legal reviewer: confirm applicable requirements, rights workflow, vendor relationships, retention, children/minor language and final disclosures. No applicability conclusion is inferred solely from nonprofit status or vendor templates.
- Technical verifier: prove deployed behavior matches approved wording on the actual SiteGround launch configuration. Live integrations remain off/mock until separately authorized and verified.
- Only after these approvals: implement exact agreed edits in a separate reviewed change, refresh source-parity baselines intentionally, verify EN/ES copy and functions, then authorize publication. Do not publish worksheets, unresolved placeholders or future chatbot practices as current policy.
